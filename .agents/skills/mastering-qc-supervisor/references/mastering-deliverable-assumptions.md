# Mastering and deliverable assumptions

## Freeze the requirement, not a default

A mastering plan starts from a supplied delivery specification. Record each expected value, unit, and source separately. Resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio, duration, channel count, channel layout, caption mode, title requirements, container, codec, color/display path, and audio thresholds are project or destination requirements. Do not substitute a familiar broadcast, cinema, festival, platform, or web preset when the destination has not supplied one.

If a threshold is missing, write a review criterion that requests the requirement, keep the check `planned`, and list the gap under `uncertainties`. This keeps the artifact useful without presenting a convention as a verified obligation.

## Master identity and history

Give each supplied master version its own `<UNIT>-MV###` identity, source reference, human label, and checksum when supplied. Labels such as `final`, `r2`, or a filename do not prove content, completeness, or identity. The current master pointer selects the only version that current check, deliverable, and approval evidence may support.

Historical reports and approvals remain valuable records. Mark them `historical` or `superseded`; never rewrite their version or transfer their result to the current master.

## Deliverable inventory

Inventory the required picture/sound master, caption or title sidecars, audio stems, QC report, checksum, and any destination-specific item. `present` means only that an item was supplied. `verified` requires evidence that the item opens, belongs to the current version, and matches the declared inventory or relationship. The verified QC report identifies a substantive report or record whose normalized source is distinct from the current master; a master path with a query or fragment appended is still the master, not a QC report. A missing required deliverable keeps readiness `not-ready`.

Human approval is its own record. Bind it to the exact current master version and stated review scope. Approval of picture only, a prior version, or a draft caption file does not approve the complete delivery-master-and-QC scope.

This skill prepares and validates records only. Encoding, rendering, file transfer, upload, distribution, and rights or legal clearance remain external authorized work.
