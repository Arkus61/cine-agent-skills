# Mastering and QC Supervisor

Create `mastering-qc-plan.json`; do not render, encode, measure unavailable media, alter media, upload, distribute, or approve for another person.

1. Copy the exact project and unit IDs. Register current and historical masters as `<UNIT>-MV###`, checks as `<UNIT>-QC###`, deliverables as `<UNIT>-DL###`, and evidence as `<PROJECT>-QE###`. Resolve populated title `<UNIT>-TT###`, sound `<UNIT>-PS###`, color `<UNIT>-CL###`, and VFX `<UNIT>-FX###` references even when a check remains planned.
2. Freeze supplied requirements before recording observations. Each criterion states a metric, `supplied` or `unresolved` state, operator, expected value, units, and specification source. Do not invent codec, container, display, safe-area, loudness, true-peak, or vendor thresholds. An unresolved criterion uses `review` and cannot pass or fail. Record missing requirements as assumptions or uncertainties and keep affected checks planned. Read [mastering and deliverable assumptions](references/mastering-deliverable-assumptions.md).
3. Include every required check category from the template. Resolution, frame rate, aspect ratio, duration, channel count, channel layout, sync, missing/duplicate/black frames, video levels, clipping, sample peak, true peak, loudness, captions, titles, safe area, readability, compression artifacts, VFX, color, and deliverable integrity remain distinct checks. Channel count does not establish layout; sample peak does not establish true peak, loudness, or clipping.
4. Use `planned` without applicable evidence. A supplied measurement or inspection may produce `pass` or `fail` only when it identifies the exact check, complete criterion, observed value and units, and current master version. Declare evidence provenance as `supplied-measurement-report`, `supplied-inspection-record`, `supplied-deliverable-record`, or `supplied-human-decision` according to its kind. The evidence source must identify that report or record and must not equal the master file source after path normalization and removal of query or fragment decorations. A requirement, filename, master label, or another metric is not evidence. Every evidence record remains internally well-formed; every `applicable` record additionally belongs to the current version and is bound to its target. Mark old evidence `historical` or `superseded`; never copy an r1 result or approval to r2. Read [technical QC](references/technical-qc.md).
5. Make machine-comparable results agree with `equals`, `less-than-or-equal`, `greater-than-or-equal`, `present`, or `absent`. Frame rate and duration use finite values greater than zero; channel count is a positive integer; sample peak, true peak, and loudness use other finite numeric operands, never booleans or numeric strings. Resolution, aspect ratio, and channel layout use non-empty strings. Supplied presence checks use `present` or `absent` with boolean values: `present` requires both expected and observed `true`, while `absent` requires both `false`. Contradictory operator/value pairs are invalid, and pass/fail disagreement with that semantics must be diagnosed. An unresolved presence requirement may remain `review`. Invalid operands cannot be treated as an unreviewed comparison. Use `review` for evidence-backed qualitative inspection. `human-review` means the check remains unresolved and blocks readiness when required.
6. Inventory substantive deliverables. `verified` requires exact current-master evidence; presence alone is not integrity. A verified QC report must identify its own report or record and remain distinct from the normalized current-master source. Readiness is separate from schema validity: every required category must exist, the readiness lists must equal all required checks and deliverables, every required check must pass, every required deliverable must be verified, and human approval must bind the exact current version and `delivery-master-and-qc` scope.
7. Validate against `schemas/mastering-qc-plan.schema.json`. With specifications only, produce useful criteria and review plans, leave observations empty, and return `not-ready`. With partial metadata, pass only fields that the supplied report actually demonstrates and keep all remaining checks planned.

Record assumptions and uncertainties explicitly. A valid plan is not proof that a master passed QC.
