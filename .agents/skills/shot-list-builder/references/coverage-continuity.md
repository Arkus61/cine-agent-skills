# Coverage and Continuity

## Coverage strategy

Choose a strategy before listing shots:

- **Master plus coverage:** reliable geography and editorial flexibility.
- **Evolving oner:** performance and spatial continuity with fewer edit options.
- **Subjective fragments:** controlled information and psychological alignment.
- **Montage:** compressed time or associative construction.
- **Hybrid:** combine only with a clear transition rule.

## Editorial handles

Allow useful action or silence before and after the essential moment. Avoid cutting every shot so tightly that the editor cannot shape rhythm.

## Continuity checks

Track:

- line of action and screen direction;
- eyeline height and direction;
- matching body and prop action;
- doors, liquids, smoke, screens, and practical lights;
- wardrobe and makeup state;
- background timing;
- sound perspective and room tone.

## Lens assumptions

Focal length depends on sensor format and camera distance. When the format is unknown, label the number as an indicative full-frame-equivalent suggestion and prioritize the desired perspective in words.
