# Camera Movement Taxonomy

## Static

Use a locked or controlled static frame when tension comes from internal change, composition, performance, or waiting. Static does not mean emotionally neutral.

## Rotational moves

- **Pan:** rotate horizontally from a fixed position.
- **Tilt:** rotate vertically from a fixed position.
- **Whip pan:** very fast pan that creates blur and may bridge space or time.

## Translational moves

- **Dolly in or out:** move toward or away from the subject, changing spatial relationship.
- **Truck left or right:** move laterally while maintaining orientation.
- **Pedestal up or down:** raise or lower the camera without tilting.
- **Crane or jib:** move through vertical and horizontal space on an arm.
- **Orbit or arc:** curve around a subject, changing background and relationship.

## Follow systems

- **Handheld:** operator-supported instability; use degree and rhythm intentionally.
- **Stabilized follow:** gimbal or Steadicam-style movement that travels with the subject.
- **Vehicle or process rig:** movement mechanically tied to another moving platform.

## Compound movement

Combine movements only when a single family cannot express the required change. State which component leads and where acceleration changes.

## Focus and zoom

Rack focus and optical zoom are not physical camera movement. Record them separately when used. A dolly zoom combines physical translation with focal-length change and requires technical planning, not merely a dramatic adjective.
