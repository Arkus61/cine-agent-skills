# Cut motivation

A cut is a change in audience attention. Name the change it serves before naming the technique. Several motivations can cooperate, but do not list every category to disguise an arbitrary transition.

## Decision order

1. Identify the new emotion, story information, relationship, question, or physical consequence the audience must register.
2. Identify where attention currently rests and where it must move.
3. Find the supplied image, movement, or sound event that can carry that change.
4. Choose the least elaborate transition that expresses the relationship.
5. Record what would make the cut earlier, later, or unnecessary when media is inspected.

## Motivation dimensions

| Dimension | Use it when | Check before committing |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion | A reaction, withheld response, or changed feeling is the scene's event. | Does the cut reveal or interrupt the intended experience? |
| Story | New information, causality, reversal, or consequence changes understanding. | Can a viewer state what changed after the cut? |
| Rhythm | Pressure, release, repetition, surprise, or silence needs a new pulse. | Is the rhythm driven by meaning rather than a fixed duration? |
| Eye trace | Attention can travel from one screen area or subject to another. | Is the intended landing point supported by supplied composition evidence? |
| Screen plane | Geography, scale, direction, or spatial relation must remain clear or deliberately rupture. | Is the axis known, or only planned? |
| Movement | Gesture, action, camera motion, or changing energy can connect shots. | Is motion actually supplied or inspected, and is direction compatible? |
| Sound | Dialogue, ambience, effect, music, or silence can prepare or prolong an image change. | Is the sound source supplied, and what narrative work does the overlap do? |

## Restraint

- A reaction cut needs a supplied reaction shot or reviewed media item. Do not infer one from a screenplay beat.
- A match cut needs a meaningful graphic or motion relation, not merely two vaguely similar objects.
- A dissolve or fade signals a perceptible relation or boundary; it is not polish for an unmotivated cut.
- A duration range is a proposal, not source evidence. Prefer event-based intent until playable media is available.
- Sound can motivate a cut without proving that the required production sound exists. Keep unavailable sound in uncertainty or downstream sound-post intent.
