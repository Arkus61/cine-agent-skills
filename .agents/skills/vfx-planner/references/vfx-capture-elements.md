# VFX capture and authored elements

## Route by shot condition

Capture planning states what evidence or element is needed, not which device, product, application, or provider creates it.

| Condition | Appropriate plan |
|---|---|
| Photographed effect | Decide plates, tracking, camera/lens/light metadata, references, interaction, mattes, and safety review as applicable. |
| Hybrid shot | Separate photographed and authored contributions and the evidence needed to align them. |
| Fully animated shot | Waive photographic plates and live capture with reasons; request authored references, holdouts, state data, and simulation evidence. |
| Invisible cleanup | Plan revealed background, retained edges/contact/shadow, tracking decision, and temporal acceptance. |
| No-VFX shot | Record no-VFX applicability only; manufacture no capture tasks. |

## Clean plates

Every effect makes a structured decision: `required`, `waived`, or `unresolved`. Required plates identify the background, set, surface, or performance-free area to preserve and the matching state that matters—camera, framing, focus, exposure, lighting, reflection, shadow, atmosphere, or reset. Waived plates state why none applies, as in a fully animated shot. Unresolved need links to an owned uncertainty. “Get a plate” without the protected relationship is not actionable.

## Tracking and metadata

Tracking can concern camera, object, performer, surface, or their relationship. `required` includes nonempty observable needs; `not-required` includes none; `unresolved` links uncertainty. State stable features across the usable range, camera/framing change, driving motion, scale and orientation, contact points, occlusion loss, and relevant start/end state.

The [ScreenSkills data capture technician profile](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/visual-effects-vfx/on-set/data-capture-technician/) connects on-set data work with VFX supervision, matchmove, and marker needs. The [ScreenSkills matchmove artist profile](https://www.screenskills.com/job-profiles/browse/visual-effects-vfx/computer-generated/matchmove-artist/) similarly connects marker planning, data capture, and live-action crew. The planning consequence is to state what must be recoverable and route marker placement to human review, never prescribe physical placement on people or sets.

Repeat supplied camera, lens, and lighting metadata exactly and scope each record to one effect shot. Each effect shot needs one claim in each of those three categories, and a supplied claim repeats its `(category, value, source_reference)` source record exactly. An approved claim instead links to exact structured approval evidence; missing values remain uncertain and must not be inferred from appearance.

## Mattes, holdouts, and references

A matte or holdout names the subject and depth relationship it preserves: hand over hologram, hair against extension, reflection over replacement, or a prop crossing behind and in front of an authored element. For animation, the same concept applies to authored separations and depth ordering without assuming photographed extraction.

Each reference request answers a question: surface response, scale, articulation, contact, interaction, approved design, state, motion, lighting, or stylization. Fully animated work can use authored design and motion references; it does not need fabricated live capture. “More reference” is insufficient—state the gap it closes and its owning shot/effect.

## Practical interaction and elements

Plan only applicable causal responses: contact, displacement, performer reaction, eyeline, illumination, shadow, reflection, atmosphere, surface change, or secondary motion. Preserve supplied performance. A hologram glitch binds to the supplied flinch; cable cleanup preserves the key, still hand, locked framing, warm lamp, and hard contact shadow.

List the smallest complete element set: clean background, tracked plate, surface reference, camera/lens record, approved design, foreground separation, authored effect element, contact reference, shadow/reflection reference, or continuity record. The plan names expected elements; it does not embed media, encoded data, URLs, credentials, or transfer instructions.

## Uncertainty and handoff check

Boundary, plate, tracking, reference, capture, and simulation questions remain unresolved until project evidence settles them. Each unresolved record links to a positive project-owned uncertainty that lists the same effect and shots and names a human owner; the effect lists the same uncertainty ID back. A confirmed simulation carries exact source-bound approval evidence and no uncertainty. A confirmed boundary carries either exact approval evidence or exact supplied-boundary evidence, never both; the supplied record must use an existing source-context reference and repeat the exact practical and digital scopes. `not-required` carries neither requirements nor uncertainty.

Before handoff verify source-bound camera/lens/light metadata, reasoned plate and tracking decisions, purpose-owned matte/reference/interaction/element needs, no forced live capture for animation, bidirectional uncertainty ownership, observable vendor-neutral acceptance criteria, and qualified human review for possible human impact.
