# Voice, music, and sound prompts

Voice and dialogue prompts describe supplied speaker/source, performance intent, timing, and an original, non-identifiable boundary. A real-person request without supplied consent evidence triggers a qualified-human consent/rights review handoff, never a clearance claim.

Music prompts state dramatic function, dialogue policy, entry/exit shape, texture, and originality boundary. Ambience, Foley, and `effect` prompts are sound-effect prompts: state source, event or bed, perspective, timing, decay, exclusions, and acceptance checks. Put visual VFX instructions in image/key-frame/video content or the VFX artifact, never in sound fields. Do not imitate living artists or invent voice, demographic, or musical traits.

| Family | Required `{value, provenance}` fields |
| --- | --- |
| Voice/dialogue | speaker_or_source, performance_intent, delivery_timing, originality_boundary |
| Music | dramatic_function, dialogue_policy, entry_exit_shape, instrumental_texture, originality_boundary |
| Ambience/Foley/effect | sound_source, event_or_bed, perspective, timing_and_decay |
