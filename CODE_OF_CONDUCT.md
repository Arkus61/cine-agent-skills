# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want contributions to be technically rigorous, source-conscious, and
welcoming. Participants should communicate with respect, assume good intent,
give actionable feedback, and make room for different levels of film and
software experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific, especially when reviewing creative or technical
  decisions.
- Separate supplied facts, assumptions, proposals, and evidence.
- Credit sources and respect copyright, privacy, and licensing boundaries.
- Accept correction without retaliation and help others reproduce problems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, deliberate
exposure of secrets or private material, plagiarism, and attempts to disguise
unsupported claims as evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub security or
maintainer contact channel. Maintainers may clarify, remove, or reject content
that violates this code, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep
the project safe and constructive.
