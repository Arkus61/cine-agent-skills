# Ninel Scene Brief

## Scene ID

S01

## Context

Ninel, a humanoid android, wakes inside a dark autonomous research ship orbiting an unknown fantasy world. She does not yet know what she is. The ship intelligence Argo can speak but withholds part of the mission.

## Scene

Emergency light pulses across a sealed medical bay. Ninel opens her eyes but does not breathe. A diagnostic voice counts down from ten. At seven, she forces a breath after noticing frost move on the glass above her.

The chamber opens. She sits up too quickly and catches her reflection in a black monitor. For a moment the reflection continues looking at her after she has turned away. The monitor clears before she can confirm it.

ARGO, heard through the room: “Welcome back, Ninel.”

She asks, “Back from where?”

A pause. The ship rotates, and a blue-green planet enters the observation slit behind her.

Argo answers, “From a failure.”

## Constraints

- One performer on screen.
- Argo is voice-only.
- Compact set with one chamber, one monitor, and one narrow observation slit.
- Target duration: 45-60 seconds.
- Tone: restrained science fiction with unease, not horror.
- Camera package assumption: full-frame equivalent focal lengths.
