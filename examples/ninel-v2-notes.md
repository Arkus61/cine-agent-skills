# Ninel v2 integration demonstration

This checked-in package demonstrates structural integration for `series` with
`animation` and `ai`. It is not an approved screenplay, season, production design,
creative evaluation or completed pilot. The source grounding is the existing
`ninel-v1/scenes/S01/source-scene.md`: Ninel is a humanoid android awakening in a
research ship medical bay; Argo is voice-only; the chamber, uncertain reflection
and observation slit are present. No new creative canon is asserted here.

Two very short demonstration units divide awakening from Argo's answer. This
division, their 30-second planning estimates, four shots, emotional interpretation,
season framing and all further development are authored proposals. The post
package covers E01 only; E02 has script, scene and production planning. The
minimal scenes are integration material, not a claim of a fully authored pilot.
